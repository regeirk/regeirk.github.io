About

I build data and machine-learning systems that turn complex, high-volume data into reliable products and better decisions.

I am a Senior Data Engineer at Bloomberg, where I work at the intersection of data engineering, machine learning, financial markets, and product development. My experience spans energy, macroeconomics, fixed income, equities, and commodities, with a focus on building production systems that make complex financial data more useful, reliable, and accessible.

My career has followed a common thread: using quantitative methods to make sense of complex systems. I started in physics, moved into oceanography and scientific computing, and eventually applied those skills to machine learning, data engineering, and financial markets.

Today, I am particularly interested in problems where science, data, technology, and human decision-making intersect, especially when research-heavy or ambiguous problems can be transformed into robust, scalable, production-grade systems.

Core expertise

  • Financial data: Macroeconomics, fixed income, equities, commodities, energy, and alternative data
